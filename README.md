# EduEquity_Kenya
### Data-driven platform for assessing school resource needs and supporting equitable education resource allocation in Kenya.
## Project Overview
EduEquity Kenya is a data-driven education equity platform designed to identify differences in school resource needs and support more informed, evidence-based resource allocation across schools in Kenya.

## Problem Statement
Education resources are not distributed evenly across schools, and differences in enrollment, teacher availability, learning materials, infrastructure, ICT access, WASH facilities, geographic conditions, and socioeconomic circumstances can create substantial differences in school needs.

However, education resource allocation requires reliable evidence about where the greatest needs exist and which factors contribute to those needs. EduEquity Kenya seeks to address this challenge by integrating relevant education and contextual data to assess school resource needs and provide data-driven insights that can support more equitable decision-making.

## Project Objectives
The project aims to:

- Analyze differences in education resources and school conditions across Kenya.
- Identify factors associated with higher levels of school resource need.
- Develop a Resource Need Index (RNI) to help assess and prioritize school needs.
- Provide data-driven insights that can support education resource allocation.
- Present findings in an accessible format for education stakeholders and decision-makers.
- Develop a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) component that can provide contextual explanations based on the project's data and knowledge base.

## Key Stakeholders
The project is designed to provide insights that may be useful to:

- **Ministry of Education:** National education planning and resource allocation.
- **County and Sub-County Education Offices:** Local education planning and identification of priority areas.
- **School Administrators:** Understanding school-level resource gaps.
- **Teachers and Education Agencies:** Supporting teacher and learning-resource planning.
- **Development Partners and NGOs:** Identifying areas where education interventions may have the greatest need.
- **Researchers and Policy Analysts:** Supporting evidence-based research on education equity in Kenya.

## Data Sources
The project will integrate publicly available education, demographic, socioeconomic, geographic, and school-level data from credible sources.

Potential data sources include:

- Ministry of Education
- Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS)
- Teachers Service Commission (TSC)
- Other relevant government agencies
- Open government datasets
- Publicly available research and development datasets

Specific datasets, variables, data availability, and licensing conditions will be documented as the data collection phase progresses.
